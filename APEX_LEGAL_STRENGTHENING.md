# APEX Legal Strengthening

Legal source-strengthening layer. Preserve source identity, provenance, court/date metadata, temporal applicability, and verification state in all case-law or docket data consumed downstream. Retrieval confidence is not legal validity; contradictions and unresolved authority state remain explicit. Companion: `GlacierEQ/apex-control-plane/legal/APEX_LEGAL_STRENGTHENING_V1.md`.
